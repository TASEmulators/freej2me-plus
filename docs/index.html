J2ME - PLUS

A fork of FreeJ2ME, a free, cross-platform J2ME emulator with support for Libretro and AWT frontends.

Compatibility list

FreeJ2ME-Plus is capable of running many J2ME apps and games, but it is still far from being 100% accurate. You can find its official compatibility list right below.

J2ME Docs

FreeJ2ME-Plus aims to implement all relevant J2ME Specifications, which go from the common MIDP profiles and related JSRs to vendor-specific APIs from western vendors like Nokia, Siemens, Motorola, and eastern vendors such as SoftBank, NTT DoCoMo, etc.

In case you want to have a look at their javadocs, they're accessible by clicking the button.
